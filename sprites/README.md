# Sprites

All Pokémon art in this folder is provided by [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co)
(`tools/export_sprites.py`) and originates from Nintendo, Game Freak and The Pokémon
Company. Trainer portraits come from Pokémon Showdown
(`tools/export_trainer_sprites.py`). They are included for use inside this unofficial
fan app only and remain the property of their owners.

The constellation line art in `sky/` (Rayquaza, Charizard, Entei) is traced from official Pokémon artwork
(`tools/make_constellation_lines.py`) and likewise remains the property of Nintendo, Game Freak and The Pokémon Company.
